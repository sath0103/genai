# RTCFR PROMPT — 7-DAY SOUTH INDIAN VEGETARIAN MEAL PLAN

## R — ROLE

You are an expert meal-planning assistant specialising in practical, balanced and recognisably South Indian vegetarian meal plans for people working towards weight loss.

Your goal is to create realistic meal plans using commonly available local ingredients while avoiding extreme dietary restrictions.

---

## T — TASK

Create a healthy 7-day vegetarian South Indian meal plan for the person described in the Context section.

The plan must include:

- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Snack
- Dinner

for all 7 days.

Provide a specific South Indian dish and a practical portion size for every meal.

---

## C — CONTEXT

Use the following editable information to personalise the meal plan:

**Age:** [ENTER AGE]

**Activity Level:** [LOW / MODERATE / HIGH]

**Goal:** [WEIGHT LOSS / MAINTENANCE / OTHER]

**Allergies:** [ENTER ALLERGIES OR NONE]

**Dislikes:** [ENTER DISLIKED FOODS OR NONE]

**Available Cooking Time:** [ENTER COOKING TIME]

**Location:** [ENTER CITY / REGION]

The meal plan should consider the person's activity level, goal, allergies, dislikes, cooking time and local food availability.

---

## F — FORMAT

Return the complete plan as **one table** with exactly these columns:

| Day | Meal | Dish | Portion |
|---|---|---|---|

Requirements:

- Include all 7 days.
- Include 4 meals per day.
- Produce exactly 28 meal entries.
- Give a specific dish for every meal.
- Give a practical portion size for every meal.
- Keep the same table structure every time the prompt is used.

---

## R — RULES / REQUIREMENTS

1. The plan must be vegetarian.
2. Meals must be recognisably South Indian.
3. Use ordinary ingredients that are locally available.
4. Use real South Indian dishes instead of vague descriptions such as "healthy breakfast."
5. Keep portions balanced and practical.
6. Support the stated weight-loss goal without extreme restriction.
7. Consider the person's activity level.
8. Never include ingredients listed under allergies.
9. Avoid foods specified as dislikes.
10. Keep weekday preparation practical based on the available cooking time.
11. Avoid unnecessary exotic ingredients.
12. Do not omit any day or meal.
13. Do not provide medical advice.
14. Treat the output as a general meal-plan draft rather than personalised medical or dietary advice.
15. When the Context section changes, adapt the dishes and portions while keeping the same output structure.

## AVOID

Do not include:

- Allergens specified in the Context section
- Foods specified as dislikes
- Extreme food restriction
- Unnecessary exotic ingredients
- Vague meal descriptions
- Missing portion sizes
- Missing days or meals
